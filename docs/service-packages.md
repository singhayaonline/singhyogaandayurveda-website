# Singhaya Online Service Packages

## Brand

**SINGHAYA ONLINE**  
Digital Ecosystem for Wellness Brands

This document defines the service packages for Singhaya Online. These packages are designed for wellness brands, yoga studios, Ayurveda businesses, retreats, cafés, clinics, and small business owners who need digital support, online marketing, and backend system setup.

---

### Best For

**3,000 THB / month**  
Minimum contract: **6 months**

- Basic social media content planning
- Facebook / Instagram page support
- Basic website checkup
- Basic SEO review
- Monthly content direction
- Monthly work summary
- Basic consultation

### Suitable For

- Small yoga studios
- Healthy cafés
- Local wellness businesses
- Small service businesses
- Businesses that already have a website or social media page
