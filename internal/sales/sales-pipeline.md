# Singhaya Online Sales Pipeline

## Purpose

This file is used to track potential clients, proposal status, follow-up dates, and monthly revenue opportunities for Singhaya Online.

## Pipeline Stages

| Stage | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Lead | New potential client identified |
| Discovery | Initial discussion or business review started |
| Proposal | Proposal prepared or sent |
| Negotiation | Price, scope, or timeline under discussion |
| Confirmed | Client agreed to proceed |
| Active | Work has started |
| Completed | Project or service period completed |
| Lost / Paused | Client did not proceed or project paused |

## Monthly Revenue Target

### Entry Target

10 clients x 3,000 THB/month = 30,000 THB/month recurring revenue

### Growth Target

- Starter Digital Care

## Follow-Up Rule

Every lead should have:

- Date contacted
- Client problem
- Recommended solution
- Proposal status
- Follow-up date
- Next action
