## 6. Payment Tracking Rule

For every active client, track:

- Monthly fee
- Contract term
- Payment due date
- Payment received date
- Outstanding balance
- Renewal date
- Notes for follow-up

---

## 7. Internal Finance Rule

Do not store bank account details, payment slips, personal financial documents, customer private information, or confidential contract files in GitHub.

This file should only track business revenue status and payment notes.

---

## 8. Growth Strategy

Singhaya Online should prioritize recurring monthly revenue before investing in:

- New computers
- Self-hosted servers
- Paid software
- Hiring team members
- Custom platform development

Cash flow should come first. Infrastructure should grow after stable recurring income is achieved.
