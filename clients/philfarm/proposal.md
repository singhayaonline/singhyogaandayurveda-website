# Proposal: PhilFarm Garden Kitchen & Wellness Café

Prepared by: **SINGHAYA ONLINE**  
Tagline: **Digital Ecosystem for Wellness Brands**

---

## 1. Project Overview

PhilFarm Garden Kitchen & Wellness Café has strong potential to grow as a nature-based café, garden destination, wellness activity space, and community lifestyle brand in Chiang Mai.

Singhaya Online proposes to support PhilFarm with affordable monthly digital marketing, online communication, content planning, and basic business system structure to help improve brand clarity, customer engagement, and sales opportunities.

---

## 2. Current Situation

PhilFarm already has a physical location, food and beverage potential, staff, garden atmosphere, and wellness event opportunities.

However, the business may benefit from clearer brand positioning, more consistent social media communication, stronger content direction, and a basic customer inquiry system to support monthly growth.

---

## 3. Main Challenges

- Online communication is not yet consistent
- Brand concept needs to be clearer
- Menu and wellness direction need better presentation
- Social media content needs monthly planning
- Customer inquiry flow should be more organized
- Need to attract café customers, tourists, cyclists, wellness clients, and event groups
- Need to increase cash flow with affordable monthly support
- Need a simple digital system before expanding into booking or CRM

---

## 4. Recommended Direction

We recommend positioning PhilFarm as:

**PhilFarm Garden Kitchen & Wellness Café**

A warm garden café and lifestyle destination that connects food, nature, wellness, community, and local Chiang Mai experiences.
