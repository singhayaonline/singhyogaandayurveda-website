# Client Brief: PhilFarm

## Client / Project Name

PhilFarm Garden Kitchen & Wellness Café

## Project Type

Digital Marketing, Online Presence, Menu Concept Support, Wellness Brand Positioning, and Business System Setup

---

## Current Situation

PhilFarm is a café, garden, and wellness-related venue with potential to grow into a lifestyle, food, and wellness destination.

The business has a physical location, existing staff, food and beverage potential, and space for wellness activities. However, the business needs clearer brand positioning, stronger online communication, consistent content, and a structured marketing system to support sales and customer engagement.

---

## Main Business Challenges

- Online communication is not yet clear
- Brand concept needs refinement
- Menu direction needs stronger positioning
- Social media content needs consistency
- Customer journey is not fully structured
- Need to increase sales and cash flow
- Need to attract local customers, tourists, cyclists, wellness customers, and event groups
- Staff and business workflow may need clearer structure
- Need monthly marketing support with affordable cost

---

## Target Customers

- Local Chiang Mai customers
- Tourists
- Cyclists
- Wellness lifestyle customers
- Yoga and retreat customers
- Café and garden visitors
- Healthy food customers
- Small event groups
- Workshop participants

---

## Recommended Brand Direction

PhilFarm can be positioned as:

**Garden Kitchen & Wellness Café**

A warm, nature-based café and lifestyle destination that connects food, garden, wellness, community, and local experience.
